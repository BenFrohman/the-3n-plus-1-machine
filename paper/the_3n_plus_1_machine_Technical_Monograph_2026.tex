\begin{abstract}
Standalone TypeScript/React/Node computational platform (`src/`, `server/`, `shared/`, `migrations/`) for interactive exploration of 3n+1 Collatz trajectories, 2-adic parity-vector trees, Terras stopping-time distributions, and cycle-equation invariants.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, Scientific Honesty, and Non-Claim Boundary}
Computational and visual exploration platform for verified finite-range Collatz dynamics and algebraic cycle identities; does not claim an unconditional proof of the global 3n+1 conjecture.
